05 / FAQ

Got questions?
We've got answers.

How do I join RestrictedRP?+

Join the official Discord, read the server information, and apply through the whitelist section to get to join our great city.

Do I need to be experienced at roleplay?+

No. New players are welcome. We provide guidance and resources so you can learn the basics and start creating good RP.

Is RestrictedRP serious roleplay?+

Yes. The server is designed around immersive roleplay and clear rules while still leaving room for players to have fun.

Where can I get support?+

Our Support Discord is the main place for player support, announcements, reports, and community communication.
